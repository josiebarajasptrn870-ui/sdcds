Privacy policy

We treat your data like a good sourdough starter — with care, patience, and never too much handling.
📅 Last updated: April 2026 · (we review this policy as often as we refresh our starter)

Our commitment

At Baking Bliss Corner, we believe that privacy is as essential as a good oven spring. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or interact with us. We are a small, independent baking journal — not a data factory.

What we collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide:

  • Contact form — name, email address, and message content.
  • Newsletter sign‑up — email address (if you choose to subscribe).
  • Cookies — we use minimal cookies to remember your preferences and for basic analytics (see below).

How we use your data

  • To reply to your messages and baking questions.
  • To send you occasional updates if you have opted in.
  • To improve our content and understand which recipes you love most.

We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Ever.

Cookies & analytics

We use a lightweight, privacy‑respecting analytics tool to count page visits and see which posts are popular. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising. You can disable cookies in your browser at any time.

  • Essential cookies: keep the site functioning.
  • Preference cookies: remember your settings.
  • Analytics cookies: aggregate, anonymised data.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (if you’ve subscribed).

To exercise any of these rights, simply drop us a line — we’ll respond within 48 hours.

Data security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. Our site uses HTTPS, and we store data on secure servers. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure — we do our best, just like we do with our meringues.

Third‑party links

Our articles may include links to other websites (e.g., ingredient suppliers, cookbooks). We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so we encourage you to read their policies.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We’ll always post the latest version here, with the date at the top. If we make significant changes, we’ll notify you via a notice on our homepage.

🍞 Questions about privacy?

Reach out to our privacy@bakingbliss.com — we’re happy to explain anything in plain language.

This policy is baked with transparency. No fancy legal jargon — just honest ingredients.
