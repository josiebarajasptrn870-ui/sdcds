    The art of the overnight sourdough

    by Eleanor Hart · 6 min read · updated 12 Apr 2026

    There is a quiet alchemy in sourdough: water, flour, salt, and time. But the overnight bulk fermentation is where depth of flavour is born. This guide walks through the subtle signs — the slight doming, the web of gluten, the gentle give of the dough — that tell you when to shape and when to chill.

    With a simple schedule that fits into any day, you’ll learn to read your starter, adjust for temperature, and bake a loaf that crackles with character.

    📊 baker’s ratio & timeline
    • 🌾 100% flour (strong white)
    • 💧 72% water (filtered, room temp)
    • 🧂 2% fine sea salt
    • ⏳ 20% active starter (peak)
    • 🌙 overnight cold proof (12–16h)
    ⏱️ total time: ~18h · active work: ~45min
